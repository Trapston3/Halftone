## Variant: Ink — monochrome editorial

### Design stance
The cover is the interface: art-as-hero with every control pushed to the edges, Apple-Music-quiet chrome, ink-on-black precision.

### Key choices
- Layout: 312px column, 312px square art bleeding to the top edges; controls pinned to the bottom rhythm
- Seek/visualizer: one 8px hairline strip of spectrum bars that grows to 36px on hover — the bars ARE the waveform; played side brightens, unplayed stays 18% ink; white hairline cursor
- Typography: 16/640 title + 11/590 caps artist + tabular times — three sizes total
- Depth: exactly two elevation levels (widget e1, popovers e2), one radius family (14/10/8), 4px spacing scale throughout
- Lyrics: dim ink lines that brighten to paper on the active line, center-anchored smooth auto-scroll

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: gallery calm, art-forward albums, "sits quietly on a second monitor"
- Weak at: low visual fun at rest; the signature moment is subtle until you touch it

### Best for
Users who want the album art to be the star — closest in spirit to the Apple Music / Cider discipline.
