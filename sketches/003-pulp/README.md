## Variant: Pulp — comic print

### Design stance
Comic-book print logic: hard 2px ink outlines, offset print shadows, acid neon on near-black — the widget as a drawn object, not a glass panel.

### Key choices
- Layout: 336px card, 216px halftone-dot art hero with 26/800 display title knocked out on top
- Seek/visualizer: 14px → 46px ink meter with chunky rounded bars; bars ahead of the cursor squash to 30% while dragging (the signature beat); neon ball-head cursor with ink outline
- Typography: 26/800 display on art, 15/700 title, 11/600 caps meta — three tiers, one voice
- Depth: ZERO soft shadows — one offset ink shadow per surface (6px widget, 4px popovers); buttons physically press into the page (translate 3px + shadow collapses to 0)
- Lyrics: bordered ink panel, neon active line, offset-sticker tooltip on drag

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: loudest personality, tactile press physics, instantly demo-able
- Weak at: heaviest at rest; the most opinionated — will clash with subtle wallpapers

### Best for
The "visible, demo-able differentiation" lane; matches a comic/neon product aesthetic.
